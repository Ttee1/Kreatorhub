Good to know

Agent questions, answered.

The short version of what agencies ask us most before they onboard a roster.

Visit the FAQ ->

Can agencies use Kreatorhub?

Absolutely. Set up an agency account, manage multiple creators from one dashboard, and handle everything from opportunities to payouts in one place.

How do I onboard my creators?

Each creator signs up and connects their social accounts, then accepts your connect request. After that, you can manage their profile, rates, and pitches on their behalf.

What does it cost?

Right now, it is completely free for agencies. Payments run through Stripe, and your commission is automatically split out, with your share going to your account and the rest going to the creator.

Can we set our own rates?

Yes. You can handle rates, pitches, and gifting opportunities for your creators, they just need to sign up and connect their accounts so the analytics stay current.

Can brands see which creators we represent?

Yes, brands can see exactly which creators are represented by your agency and click through to view your full roster. More roster discoverability features are on the way.
